============================================================
QUALITATIVE FINDING
============================================================

The base Qwen model showed very weak Amharic generation,
including corrupted/mixed-language output, repetition,
prompt copying, and unrelated answers.

The distilled model showed a substantial improvement in
Amharic-like generation and task-format behavior.

However, the distilled model still showed:
- repetition
- semantic errors
- incorrect answers
- occasional failure to translate
- hallucination
- incomplete summarization

Therefore the correct conclusion is NOT that distillation
solved Amharic.

The supported conclusion is:

Response-level QLoRA distillation substantially improved
the student's ability to generate Amharic-like text and
follow the general task format, but semantic accuracy
remained weak and repetitive generation persisted.
